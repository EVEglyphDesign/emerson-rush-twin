## What this means for a mirror or a takeover

- The two eras coexist on the same domain and both are reachable. A cutover has to preserve both, or make an explicit decision about which era is the go-forward surface.
- The `/es/` tree is production content but is not declared in the sitemap, which is why standard crawlers under-count the site. This mirror captured `/es/` by following internal navigation, not by trusting `sitemap.xml`.
- Consultant and seat counts differ across pages. Whichever number the client wants public should be aligned on both eras before publish.
