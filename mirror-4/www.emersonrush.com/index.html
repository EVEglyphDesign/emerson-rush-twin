Fourth mirror · Group Platforms revision

A handbook tuned for the room

Lillian’s Guide, prepared for a Group Platforms conversation inside a multi-brand SAP enterprise running a business-led, clean-core, satellites-and-BTP transformation.

The third mirror published Lillian’s Guide as the peer-review canon — the working handbook a practitioner in Lilian Corvington’s lineage might practise from without any particular reader in mind. This fourth mirror keeps the same discipline and takes one further step: the vocabulary of a Group Platforms room in a multi-brand SAP enterprise is met on the page, so a reader inside such a programme does not have to translate.

The handbook names no client and no engagement. Recognition is meant to come from the words a programme already uses about itself — clean-core, satellites and BTP, ACDOCA, Datasphere, DMZ, Decision Intelligence, business-led. If the vocabulary fits, the paper fits.
